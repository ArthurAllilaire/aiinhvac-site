Best AI for commercial refrigeration technicians in 2026

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

Commercial refrigeration is the hardest case for general AI: every store is piped differently, a failed case costs stock by the hour, and refrigerant work has to be recorded. The tools worth looking at either know the site's history or come from the manufacturer of the component.

  • Kenner: built for supermarket, food service and cold storage service teams, with the refrigerant record written to the unit's file. Our product.
  • Copeland Mobile with Scout AI: free answers from Copeland's compressor bulletins, plus refrigerant calculators.
  • Bluon MasterMechanic: HVAC-R equipment data and troubleshooting by model number.
  • BuildOps OpsAI: voice notes and asset history for commercial refrigeration contractors on BuildOps.
  • ServiceTrade Smart Assistants: a job brief from site history for commercial service contractors on ServiceTrade.
  • HVAC/R AI Pro: a low-cost app with refrigeration calculators and refrigerant logs.

What refrigeration work needs from AI

The site's history. Defrost that ends early on one case, a gas cooler fan that fails every summer, a valve that was replaced with a different part: none of it is in a manual.

The store's own data. Kenner reads store alarms and case temperatures from supervisor systems before the technician arrives.

The record. After a leak repair or a top-up, the refrigerant, the amount, where the leak was and how it was tested have to be written down. Kenner asks for exactly that, writes it to the unit's record and books the follow-up leak check. Your certified technicians still make the calls.

Questions and answers

Does any AI help with EPA 608 or F-gas records?

Kenner describes collecting what the refrigerant record needs after a leak repair or top-up and writing it to the unit's record in your field service system. HVAC/R AI Pro keeps refrigerant logs inside its app on paid plans. Neither replaces certification.

Which AI works for CO2 systems?

No vendor we checked publishes a list of supported refrigerants for its AI. A tool that learns from your own jobs will know your CO2 racks to the extent your technicians have worked on them. Ask the vendor to show it on one of your sites.
