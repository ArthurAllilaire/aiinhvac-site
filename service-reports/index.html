AI in HVAC

AI that writes HVAC service reports

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

Several tools now write the visit summary from what the technician says or photographs. The questions that matter are whether it takes a voice note, whether the result lands in your system of record without retyping, and whether it captures what the next technician will need.

  • Kenner: a voice note becomes the report, written to your FSM, ERP or CRM with parts and follow-ups. Our product.
  • BuildOps OpsAI: spoken notes become the visit summary and invoice summary in BuildOps.
  • ServiceTrade Smart Assistants: rough notes become customer-ready comments and a job summary in ServiceTrade.
  • XOi: an AI job summary and customer-ready report after every visit.
  • Bluon MasterMechanic: a job summary generated from the troubleshooting conversation.
  • measureQuick mQ Assist: reports and photos stored in its cloud, with Premier Services.

The report is also next year's answer

A good report is not only for the customer and the invoice. It is the only record of what was found and what fixed it. If it says "repaired unit", the next technician starts from zero. Tools that write the report from what the technician actually said keep the detail, and the tools that learn from past jobs can only learn what was written down.

Questions and answers

Can a technician dictate the report?

Yes. Kenner takes a voice note in the messenger the technician already uses, and BuildOps OpsAI takes dictation in its technician app.

Does the report go into our field service software?

With Kenner, yes: it writes the report, the refrigerant record, parts and follow-ups back to the FSM, ERP or CRM you run. BuildOps and ServiceTrade write to their own platforms. Check the others with the vendor.
