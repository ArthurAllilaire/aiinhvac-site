Free AI for HVAC technicians in 2026

The short answer

You can get a long way for free. Two manufacturers' apps are free outright, two chat tools have free plans, and the leading diagnostics app is free without its AI. What no free download gives you is the history of the unit or the report written for you. Kenner does both and is free while it is in beta, but it starts with a pilot for a team, not a download.

  • Copeland Mobile with Scout AI: free, with Scout AI answering from Copeland's documentation.
  • Carrier Service Technician app: free, with an AI assistant in beta and a manual search for commercial alarms.
  • ChatGPT: a free plan with photo and voice input and limits on uploads.
  • HVAC/R AI Pro: a free plan with a small weekly AI allowance that does not save chats.
  • measureQuick mQ Assist: the diagnostics app is free. The AI is in the $49 a month tier.
  • Kenner: free while in beta, for service teams. It starts with a demo and a two-week pilot, not a download.

What free covers

Theory, fault code meanings, a manufacturer's bulletins, calculators and live measurement are all available without paying. For a technician learning the trade or working alone, that is a real toolkit.

What it does not

The free apps and plans do not know your company's jobs, they do not write the service report into your system, and they limit how much you can ask. Those are the things a service company pays for, and they are where the tools built for teams earn their price.

Questions and answers

Is Kenner free?

Yes, while it is in beta, but it is not a free app. It starts with a demo and a two-week pilot for five to ten technicians, and prices for after the beta are not published. It is built for service teams, not for a single technician.

Is the free version of ChatGPT good enough for HVAC?

For general questions, yes. Check its answers against the manual, and note that the free plan limits file uploads and voice.
